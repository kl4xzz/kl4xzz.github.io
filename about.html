About Kashfee

Content creator and independent engineer based in Uttara, Dhaka. This page is the long version — background, discipline, and how the two sides of the work connect.

I treat a video edit and a welded chassis the same way — as a build, with a spec, a budget, and a deadline.

My work splits across two benches. On one side: video content, visual design, and AI-assisted creative workflows — scripting, editing, and shipping short-form content that's reached a wide audience. On the other: hands-on engineering, from competitive robotics to a full electric go-kart build running on a fixed budget in Dhaka.

Both sides run on the same process — plan the spec, work the constraint, ship the build. Content work taught me to move fast and iterate publicly. Engineering work taught me to respect tolerances and budgets that don't move. I use both instincts on every project now, regardless of which "bench" it started on.

I've also spent time volunteering with an NGO and competing in robotics and tech events — experiences that taught me to ship under real constraints, not just imagined ones. Alongside that, I train consistently in the gym with a focused muscle-building goal, which has turned into its own kind of discipline: tracking, adjusting, showing up.
